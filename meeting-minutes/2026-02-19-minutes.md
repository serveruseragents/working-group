# Visioning Session: The Wedding Ecosystem Orchestrator
**Duration:** 1 Hour
**Date and Time:** February 19, 2026 at 4pm EST
**Location:** Server User Agents Discord in the Meetings Channel

## About This Session

This session uses a single real person's business as the entry point for exploring how SUA infrastructure shows up in a dense, relationship-driven local economy.

Our subject owns a tuxedo rental shop, organizes and hosts a major wedding expo, and admins a large local wedding and event planning Facebook community. They sit at the center of a web of vendors, venues, couples, and community members, all of whom depend on trust to do business together.

We'll explore what SUA infrastructure could mean for someone in that position, both as a business owner navigating that ecosystem and as someone who could extend verified infrastructure to everyone flowing through it.

---

## Agenda

**Grounding (10 min)**
We'll introduce our subject: who they are, how their three operations connect, who depends on them, and what the daily reality of running all three looks like. If they're present, they'll speak for themselves.

**Open Exploration (40 min)**
The group digs into the use case together across two areas:

*As a business owner and community organizer:*
- Where does trust break down between vendors, couples, and the community?
- What does it cost them in time, money, or reputation when verification fails?
- What would change if the people they work with were verifiably who they say they are?

*As an ecosystem orchestrator:*
- Their Facebook community already functions as an informal trust layer. What are its limits?
- What would it mean for their expo to use SUAs?
- Could they become the anchor of a local verified wedding marketplace, and what would that unlock for them and for others?

**Capture and Close (10 min)**
We'll surface the most interesting open questions from the session and close with one forward-looking prompt: if this person had been running on SUA infrastructure for six months, what would be different?

A running use case brief for this scenario will be maintained in a shared Google Doc, linked from this group's pinned Discord forum thread. It will be updated as use cases are developed. View access is open to anyone with the link; edit access is limited to facilitators.

We'll also flag that organizational needs for this group, including stack and tooling, calendar and scheduling, meeting logistics, and related infrastructure, will be addressed asynchronously outside of session time.

## Meeting Minutes
### Server User Agent Infrastructure Vision

- Discussion focused on expanding SUA beyond individual users to groups and organizations
- Key tension: personal data sovereignty vs. group/community needs
- Proto group currently wrestling with group data stores and collective user agents
- Question: Should groups have dedicated SUAs or leverage existing server infrastructure?

### Business Applications & Data Sovereignty

- Companies should have their own user agents for:
  - Internal information management without external data leakage
  - Verifiable business credentials and authenticity proof
  - Direct customer connections bypassing intermediaries
- Inventory management example: flexible data storage within SUA framework
- Shift from cloud-dependent to device-local computing with cryptographic security

### Technical Requirements & Data Language

- Need for standardized protocols and ontologies for SUA communication
- Data language must handle:
  - Granular and semantic data layers
  - Credential verification systems
  - Cross-platform compatibility
- AI integration challenges similar to OpenClaw data sharing problems
- “Talking to aliens” problem: coordinating without pre-established standards

### Discovery & Connection Mechanisms

- Current centralized platforms (Facebook, etc.) provide discovery but extract data
- Wedding use case example: intent matching between customers and businesses
- Both sides need verification of authenticity without bad intermediaries
- Challenge: How businesses discover customer intent without centralized platforms
- Potential for SUAs to access existing platforms via APIs where available
