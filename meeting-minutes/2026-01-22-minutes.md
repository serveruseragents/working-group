## Concept and Technical Foundation

The group aligned on the philosophical grounding of SUAs, with user agency and the end-user principle as non-negotiable design constraints. SUAs are viable today through self-hosted or cloud implementations. The next step is protocol development to enable adoption across multiple independent projects. Several group members are already working on adjacent implementations; the goal is to align those efforts while lowering the barrier for new participants.

## Vision and Use Cases

Three core objectives were established for what SUAs should enable:

- Ownership of contacts, connections, and relationships free from platform lock-in
- Local data storage and genuine data ownership
- Elimination of single-company control over user relationships

A working example discussed: community mapping across Discord, Signal, and Threads using a Pinboard API and Claude integration for collaborative link sharing. This demonstrates the core value proposition of mixing and matching data sources without requiring platform permission.

## Outreach and Stakeholder Strategy

Three priority communities were identified for early engagement and user experience input:

- Indigenous communities involved in digital divide work
- Small business owners navigating social network dependency challenges
- Crypto and decentralized identity-familiar groups, particularly in APAC and regions with destabilized currencies

Outreach documentation should capture the process and stories, not just outcomes. Platform accessibility matters, and WhatsApp may be more appropriate than Discord for reaching global audiences. A market analysis is planned to support business development.
